THE 17TH LILIW GAT TAYAW TSINELAS FESTIVAL

📅 September 24-29, 2025

📍 Liliw Town Plaza, Laguna

About the Festival

The Gat Tayaw Tsinelas Festival is Liliw's premier celebration of its heritage as the Footwear Capital of Laguna. This week-long festival features street dancing, cultural presentations, trade fairs showcasing local footwear and products, food festivals, and various competitions.

Named after the legendary Gat Tayaw, the festival celebrates the town's rich shoemaking tradition while promoting local craftsmanship and tourism.
