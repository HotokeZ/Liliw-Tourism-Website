Travel Tips

🚗 Getting There

By Private Vehicle

From Manila, take SLEX to Calamba Exit, then follow signs to Liliw via Rizal or Nagcarlan route. Travel time: approximately 2-3 hours.

By Public Transport

Take a bus from Cubao or Buendia to Sta. Cruz, Laguna, then transfer to a jeepney bound for Liliw. Alternatively, take a bus to Nagcarlan and transfer to Liliw.

📅 Best Time to Visit

Festival Season (September)

Experience the vibrant Gat Tayaw Tsinelas Festival. Book accommodations early as this is peak season.

Dry Season (November to May)

Best weather for outdoor activities and visiting waterfalls. Perfect for hiking and exploring natural attractions.

💡 Local Tips

Language

Tagalog is widely spoken. Basic English is understood in tourist areas. Learning a few Filipino phrases is appreciated by locals.

Currency

Philippine Peso (₱). Bring cash as not all establishments accept cards. ATMs are available in the town center.

Respect Local Customs

Dress modestly when visiting churches. Remove shoes when entering homes. Always ask permission before taking photos of people.

Shopping Tips

Best deals on footwear are found in local shops. Sunday market offers fresh produce and local products. Don't hesitate to negotiate politely.
