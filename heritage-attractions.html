About St. John the Baptist Church

The St. John the Baptist Church, also known as the Liliw Church, is a historic Roman Catholic church built during the Spanish colonial period. This magnificent structure features stunning baroque architecture with its iconic bell tower that has become a symbol of Liliw's rich cultural heritage.

The church stands as a testament to the town's deep-rooted Catholic faith and colonial history. Its well-preserved architecture, intricate details, and historical significance make it a must-visit destination for history enthusiasts and pilgrims alike.
