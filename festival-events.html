About the Festival

The Tsinelas Festival is Liliw's most colorful celebration, honoring the town's rich heritage as the "Footwear Capital of Laguna." This vibrant festival showcases the creativity and craftsmanship of local shoemakers through street dancing, cultural performances, and exhibitions.

Held annually, the festival features elaborately decorated streets, traditional music, and dance performances that tell the story of Liliw's footwear industry. Visitors can witness the transformation of simple materials into beautiful handcrafted shoes and slippers.

Festival Highlights

  • 🎭 Street Dancing Competition
  • 👞 Footwear Exhibition & Sale
  • 🎨 Cultural Performances
  • 🍴 Food Festival featuring Local Delicacies
  • 🏛️ Historical Tours
  • 🎪 Trade Fair & Handicrafts Market

When to Visit

Date: Typically held in September

Duration: 5-7 days

Location: Town Plaza and Main Streets of Liliw
