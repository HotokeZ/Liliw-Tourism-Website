About Kilangin Falls

Kilangin Falls is a hidden gem nestled in the lush mountains of Liliw, Laguna. This stunning waterfall features crystal-clear turquoise waters cascading down moss-covered rocks, surrounded by verdant tropical vegetation. The falls offer a refreshing escape from the city and provide an excellent spot for nature lovers and adventure seekers.

The journey to Kilangin Falls involves a scenic trek through the forest, making it perfect for hiking enthusiasts. Visitors can swim in the natural pools, enjoy a picnic by the falls, or simply relax and take in the breathtaking natural beauty.
